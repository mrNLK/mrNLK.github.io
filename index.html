SourceKit

The best technical candidates often don't look strong in a keyword search. SourceKit takes a company and a role, builds a sourcing strategy around real technical work, and finds people through evidence of what they've built: open-source contributions, research, and products they've shipped. It scores candidates, surfaces strong engineers with a low public profile, and drafts outreach that references their actual work.

I've used it to hire 6 Engineers in Residence at AI Fund.
